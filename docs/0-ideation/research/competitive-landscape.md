# Competitive Landscape

**Last Updated**: 2025-12-27

## Market Overview

Facial composite software is used by law enforcement agencies worldwide to create suspect sketches based on witness descriptions. The market is dominated by a few established players, most offering proprietary, closed-source solutions.

## Market Characteristics

1. **Proprietary formats** - No industry-standard file format; each vendor uses proprietary files
2. **Closed ecosystems** - Limited interoperability between systems
3. **Training-dependent** - Most require certification/training programs
4. **Legacy technology** - Many systems built on older technology stacks
5. **High licensing costs** - Enterprise pricing targeted at government budgets

## Opportunity Areas

- Open-source alternative
- Modern tech stack (web-based, cross-platform)
- Open file format (.idkit or similar)
- Community-contributed feature libraries
- Lower barrier to entry for smaller agencies

## Naming Consideration

The name "Identikit" may cause trademark conflicts with Identi-Kit Solutions, an active business in this space. Consider alternative names:

- CompositeKit
- FaceForge
- WitnessSketch
- PortraitBuilder
- FaceCraft
- SketchBuilder
